Why this is happening

AI finds bugs faster on both sides. Defenders fix more of their own flaws. Attackers turn published flaws into working attacks faster: about 24 hours, down from about 72.

Most new bugs never get used. Attackers still pick a small set: edge devices, remote access and widely used web software.

Patching is the bottleneck. Exploited flaws are now the top cause of breaches, and the median time to fully fix one rose to 43 days.
