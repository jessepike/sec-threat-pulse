## What and why

Security Threat Pulse (STP) is a free, automated, vendor-neutral resource that tells leaders what is
happening in cybersecurity, what is real, what is noise, and what deserves attention. It serves
people and the agents that work for them.

Primary sources (CISA KEV and similar) hold facts with no business meaning. News and vendor blogs
add meaning mixed with hype and sales interest. Paid threat intelligence is built and priced for
SOC teams. STP pulls the best public sources several times a day, groups reports into events,
places each event on the evidence ladder, and gives a plain verdict with the reasoning and sources.

Stage: exploration. Build, run on live events, score, iterate. Real-user feedback comes at the
launch gate. A secondary outcome is credibility for Jesse Pike and Pike Holdings.

## Audience

Core: CISOs and security leaders. Also CEOs, boards, insurers, investors, and agents working for
them. Every event is written in three layers: headline verdict, evidence ladder, sources and data.
Out of scope: operational threat intelligence (IOCs, detection rules, SOC tooling).

## Principles

Evidence over volume · show the work (source and time on every claim) · open, versioned rules ·
say what we don't know · correct in public · keep score (track record) · vendor neutral ·
respect source terms · clear about AI (rules set verdicts; generated text is labeled) ·
same facts for people and agents.

## Scorecard

Accuracy · lead time · freshness (4+ runs a day) · coverage (95%+ of KEV additions) · clarity
(non-technical reader gets verdict and action in 30 seconds) · trust (100% sourced, rules public,
corrections within 24 hours) · agent readiness · running cost (under $50 a month).
