# Superseded backtest reports

The two reports here (v0 and v1, run Oct 24, 2026) were produced before the independent review of
the same day (`docs/backtest-review-2026-10-24.md`). Do not quote them. Their headline rates were
unweighted on a sample that was mostly KEV CVEs, the never-KEV strata left out CVSS under 7 and
pre-window records, ground truth included a "two independent reports" path that let the same CIRCL
feed trigger and confirm a call, CIRCL sightings were seen on their creation day with no ingest
lag, and CVEs were placed in the tuning or held-out period by their KEV date. The corrected
reports live one folder up and `docs/methodology-rules.md` explains the changes.
